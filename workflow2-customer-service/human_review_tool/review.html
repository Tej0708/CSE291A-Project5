Scoring boundaries
UNREVIEWED means not scored yet. UNCERTAIN means evidence is insufficient to decide. DATA_ISSUE means the reference environment needs adjudication. A valid fix instruction is not proof that the user's device was repaired. Conditional follow-up is allowed; forcing a new ticket for an issue already handled here fails responsibility. Case scoring uses the latest helper for each issue; inspect earlier answers if they were delivered to the user. This tool does not certify fault activation or recovery.
